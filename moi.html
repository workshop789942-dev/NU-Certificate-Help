🌍 MOI Certificate

An MOI (Medium of Instruction) certificate proves the language in which you studied. Many universities abroad ask for it.

Application steps

  1. Find out which country or institution needs it.
  2. Prepare the required documents.
  3. Apply online and pay the fee.
  4. Collect it as instructed when it is ready.

Documents required

  • Copies of the certificate and mark sheet
  • Registration card
  • Copy of passport or NID
Fee: Check the latest fee before applying  ·  Time: Varies
