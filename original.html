🎓 Original Certificate

The original certificate is issued directly by National University. It is needed for higher studies abroad, jobs and many government and private applications.

Application steps

  1. Fill in the application form and get the principal's signature and attestation.
  2. Scan the documents as PDF, keeping each file under 2 MB.
  3. Create an account on the National University Student Portal and choose the original certificate application.
  4. Fill in the fields marked with a star (*), upload the documents and pay the fee.
  5. When the dashboard shows Signatured, collect the certificate from the Gazipur main campus.

Documents required

  • Registration card
  • Admit card (final year)
  • Provisional certificate (the original copy must be submitted)
  • Mark sheet
  • Application form with the principal's recommendation
Fee: ৳ 606  ·  Time: Usually 20-30 days. Older batches may take 2-3 months.

FAQ

Can I apply without a provisional certificate?

No. The provisional certificate must be submitted when collecting the original, so get it first.

Can someone else collect the certificate for me?

Yes, with your authorization letter and a copy of your NID.

Do I need separate accounts for Honours and Masters?

Yes, you need a separate account for each course.
