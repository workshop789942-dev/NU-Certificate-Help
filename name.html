✏️ Name Correction

If your name is spelled wrongly on the registration card, admit card or certificate, it must be corrected first.

Application steps

  1. Identify which document has the mistake.
  2. Collect proof such as NID and SSC certificate.
  3. Submit the correction application through your college.
  4. Receive the corrected document and check it carefully.

Documents required

  • Card or certificate with the wrong name
  • Copies of NID and SSC certificate
  • Principal's recommendation
Fee: Check the latest fee before applying  ·  Time: Varies
